ASTER swap failing? Check approval, gas and liquidity

  • Insufficient gas: add native BNB to the sending wallet. ASTER and WBNB balances do not pay BNB Smart Chain gas directly.
  • Quote expired or no route: request a fresh quote, check the minimum, or try another supported output asset.
  • Transaction pending: check its hash before submitting another transfer. Confirmed on-chain deposits can still be awaiting order processing.
  • Token not visible: select the receiving chain and add the correct token contract to your wallet. A matching ticker alone is not enough.

Check your transaction: track your ASTER transfer on BscScan using the transaction hash.

ASTER swap questions, answered

03 / ANSWERS
Where can I swap ASTER?

Open the swap dashboard to check available ASTER routes. Select the receiving asset and network, then review the quote, minimum and fees before funding the order.

Do I need BNB to swap ASTER?

Yes, when sending BEP-20 ASTER from your own BNB Smart Chain wallet, you need native BNB for gas. Keep that BNB available before submitting the ASTER transfer.

Can I swap ASTER to USDT or USDC?

ASTER has USDT and USDC trading pairs. Check the dashboard for an available swap route and select the receiving network supported by your destination wallet.

How long does it take to swap ASTER?

Timing depends on deposit confirmations, order processing and the receiving network. Track the ASTER transaction hash and order status; an on-chain confirmation does not mean the output has already arrived.

What is the minimum amount to swap ASTER?

The minimum depends on the receiving asset, route and current network costs. Use the minimum shown in the current quote and send the amount specified by the order.

Why does the rate change when I swap ASTER?

ASTER prices, route liquidity and network fees change. A quote can expire before your deposit arrives. Review whether the order uses a fixed or floating rate and follow its deposit deadline.
