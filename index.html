What is Aster

Swapping on Aster

An on-chain exchange on BNB Chain

An Aster swap is a spot trade on Aster, a non-custodial on-chain exchange on BNB Chain. Aster offers spot trading and perpetual futures, and has its own token, ASTER. You trade from your own wallet instead of depositing funds with a company.

Spot and perpetuals are different

A spot swap exchanges one token for another and you end up holding the asset you bought. A perpetual is a leveraged contract on a price with funding payments and liquidation, which is a separate risk class. This page is about swaps.

Spot vs perps

Spot swaps compared with perpetuals

Both live on the same exchange, but they work differently and carry different risks. Know which one you are using before you sign.

How to swap

How to swap on Aster

Quote first, sign second

The interface shows the expected output, price impact and slippage tolerance before anything is signed. You confirm each transaction in your own wallet.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and switch it to BNB Chain.
  2. Select the token to sell and the token to buy, then enter an amount.
  3. Review the quote, route and estimated network fee in BNB.
  4. Approve the sell token once if it is a BEP-20 token and this is its first swap from this wallet.
  5. Sign the swap and wait for confirmation; the bought token arrives in your wallet.
Fees & gas

What swapping on Aster costs

Network fee in BNB

Every approval and swap is a BNB Chain transaction paid in BNB. Fees there are generally modest but still vary with network load. See the BNB Chain docs for how gas works.

Trading cost

The quote reflects exchange fees, price impact and any aggregator fee, so the amount shown is what you should expect to receive. Illiquid pairs move the price more on larger orders.

Keep some BNB

Without BNB in your wallet you cannot sign a swap, even if you hold plenty of other tokens.

Security

Staying safe on Aster

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction. This interface routes and quotes; it never holds your funds or keys.

Before you sign

  • Verify token contract addresses, including ASTER; fake tokens copy real names.
  • Confirm your wallet is on BNB Chain.
  • Approve only the amount you need and revoke unused allowances.
  • Do not use leverage or perpetuals unless you understand liquidation.
  • Smart contracts can contain bugs and exchanges can face outages, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

Aster Swap FAQ

What is Aster Swap?

It is a non-custodial interface for swapping tokens on Aster, an on-chain exchange on BNB Chain. You connect your own wallet, review a quote and sign each transaction yourself.

What is Aster?

Aster is a non-custodial on-chain exchange on BNB Chain that offers spot trading and perpetual futures. It has its own token, ASTER.

What is the ASTER token?

ASTER is the native token of the Aster exchange. As with any token, check its contract address before you trade and do not assume its price will hold.

Does this page support perpetuals?

This page is built around swaps. Perpetuals add leverage, funding payments and liquidation, so they carry much higher risk and need separate care.

Which token pays gas?

BNB pays gas for every transaction on BNB Chain, including approvals and swaps. Keep a small BNB balance in the same wallet.

Do I need to approve a token first?

Yes for BEP-20 tokens, because the router needs a one-time allowance to move them. The approval is a separate transaction with its own small fee. Native BNB needs no approval.
